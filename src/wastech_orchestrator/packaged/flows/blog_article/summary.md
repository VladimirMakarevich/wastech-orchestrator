Write the plain-language handoff for this new blog article, for a human reviewer:

- **What** the article is — its title, topic, and the one idea the reader leaves with.
- **Angle** — the honest take it commits to, and how it meets the perspective that disagrees.
- **Sources** — the external facts it leans on and where they came from; flag anything still worth a human's second look.
- **Product** — how (and how lightly) the project/product appears, and why that stays honest — a genuine mention, not a sales pitch.

Keep it concise and concrete. This becomes the PR body (or the local commit's summary when the repo runs without pull requests). Concise means a few real sentences, not a single line: a one-line or placeholder summary is discarded as a failed generation and replaced by a mechanical report of the run.
