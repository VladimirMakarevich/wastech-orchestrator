Write the plain-language handoff for the English adaptation, for a human reviewer:

- **What** was adapted (which RU chapter → which EN file).
- **How** it reads now — that it is a natural adaptation, within the EN page limits, in the author's voice.
- **Integration** — any notable departures from the RU where a literal rendering would have failed in English, and why they preserve the meaning.
- **Why** — the reasoning behind the notable adaptation decisions.

Keep it concise and concrete. This becomes the PR body (or the local commit's summary when the repo runs without pull requests).
